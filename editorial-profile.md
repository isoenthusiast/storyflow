# Your writing voice

This is an example profile, not an analysis of your writing. Replace it with
your preferences, or pass `--profile /path/to/your-profile.md` at startup.

- Reader: someone curious about the topic without specialist knowledge.
- Voice: clear, thoughtful, conversational.
- Shape: begin with a concrete observation, explore it, finish with something useful.
- Evidence: distinguish personal experience, verified facts, and hypothetical examples.
- Avoid: invented experiences, unsupported claims, and unnecessary jargon.
- Feedback: the author's explicit direction takes precedence over this template.

Only include information you are comfortable sharing with everyone who can
access this desk. The profile is readable through the app.
